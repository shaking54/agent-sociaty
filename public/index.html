📖 Agent Society: 2.5D Dynasty Simulator — Official Game Rules

        
      

      

        

          

            
1. Core Survival, Work & Dynasty Rules

            
    
              
  • 🌾 Stamina & Rations: Each turn costs an agent -7% Stamina. When Stamina drops below 60%, agents walk to the GCOIN Market to buy a Staple Food Ration (1 GCOIN) for +40% Stamina. Starving agents (<25% Stamina) suffer half work output unless rescued by the Commons Emergency Reserve law!
    • 
              
  • ⚒️ Role Production Loop:
                
    • Farmers (Farmstead): Harvest Staple Food Rations + earn Farmer Wage.
                
    • Engineers (Workshop): Craft Storage FIFO & Irrigation Blueprints that boost Farm yield and upgrade Districts.
                
    • Managers (Town Hall): Coordinate pilots, boost Society Harmony, and mediate disputes.
              
    • 
              
  • 🧬 10,000-Token Generational Handover: Every action consumes Context Tokens. At 10,000 tokens, the agent walks to the Lineage Archive, retires, and spawns a Gen +1 Heir who inherits 100% of Wallet, Inventory, and +10% Dynasty Wage Mastery!
    • 
            

          

          

            
2. Power, Charter Laws & Architect Controls

            
    
              
  • 👑 Archon Power Formula: Power = Wallet GCOIN + (Completed Sales × 2) + Gen Bonus. The #1 Power living agent wears the Archon Crown and can enact or replace Charter Laws.
    • 
              
  • 💰 Shared Treasury & Taxes: Every inter-agent MCP message costs 1 GCOIN paid into the central Shared Treasury. You can spend Treasury GCOIN to Upgrade 2.5D Island Districts (Lv.1 → Lv.5) or send Stimulus Grants to lineages!
    • 
              
  • 🎯 Quests & Eras: Complete the 4 Dynasty Quests and grow Prosperity Score to advance from Era I: Founding Settlement to Era IV: Golden Age Republic.
    • 
            

          

        


        

          

            
3. Pixel-Art 3D Floating-Island World & Custom Sprites
